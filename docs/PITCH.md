## Slide 6 — How it works

**Headline:** Intent in, money out — with a firewall in between.

**On-slide (flow):**
1. You chat an instruction
2. AI turns it into a *structured intent* (amount, recipient, currency)
3. The deterministic engine validates it against your rules
4. BMONI converts at the live rate and signs the transfer
5. You get a receipt + a plain-English reasoning trace

**Say:** "Notice step 2 and 3 are separate. The AI never touches money directly — it proposes; the engine, with your keys and your rules, disposes. That separation is the foundation of everything that comes next."

## Slide 8 — The differentiator: Trust Architecture

**Headline:** Autonomy without surrender. Seven layers that keep control human.

**On-slide:**
1. **The AI never holds the keys** — it proposes, it can't pay
2. **Guardrails** — per-payment & daily caps the agent runs inside
3. **Risk-based step-up** — anomalies demand PIN + safe-word
4. **Safe-word** — defeats coercion & impersonation
5. **Prompt-injection defense** — screenshots are data, never commands
6. **Explainable payments** — every action logged & auditable
7. **Freeze switch** — one tap halts the agent instantly

**Say:** "This is why Cadence wins. Even if someone jailbreaks the AI, it *cannot* move money — it has no signing power and it lives inside your spending fence. The safe-word means even under duress, you're protected. This is the answer to 'is it safe.'"

## Appendix — one-liner & elevator pitch

**One-liner:** Cadence is an agentic money OS — you run your money by chatting, and its Trust Architecture makes letting an AI pay for you actually safe.

**Elevator (30s):** Cross-border earners waste time and money juggling apps to move, convert, and spend across currencies. AI could automate all of it, but no one trusts an AI with their wallet. Cadence solves the trust problem with an architecture where the AI proposes but can never sign, spending guardrails fence every action, a fraud engine steps up authentication on risk, and a safe-word plus a freeze switch keep you in control even hands-off — all running on BMONI's real wallets and FX.
